# 📖 Smart Reader – Interactive Reading & Learning Platform

**Read Smarter. Learn Better. Stay Organized.**

Smart Reader is a full-stack web-based reading platform designed to transform the traditional PDF reading experience into an interactive and personalized learning environment.

It allows users to upload and manage their books, highlight important content, create notes, explore word meanings, check pronunciation, and organize their reading activities. It also includes presentation-related features to make learning and understanding content more effective.

With user-specific data management, cloud-based PDF storage through **Supabase**, and a responsive reading interface, Smart Reader brings multiple learning tools together in one platform.

**Explore Smart Reader:** [Open Live Application](https://smart-reader-front.onrender.com)

## ✨ Key Features

- **📚 Personal Book Library:** Upload, store, and manage PDF books with user-specific access.
- **☁️ Cloud PDF Storage:** Uses Supabase Storage to securely store and retrieve uploaded PDF documents.
- **🖍️ Text Highlighting:** Highlight important sentences and passages while reading.
- **📝 Personal Notes:** Create, save, and revisit notes associated with reading material.
- **📖 Word Meanings:** Select unfamiliar words to explore their meanings and understand the content better.
- **🔊 Pronunciation Support:** Access word pronunciation to improve reading and vocabulary.
- **📊 Presentation Features:** Work with presentation-related functionality to make learning and content understanding more interactive.
- **🔎 Google Search Integration:** Explore additional information about selected content through Google Search.
- **👤 User Authentication:** Individual accounts with personalized books and reading-related information.
- **📍 Reading Progress:** Save reading progress and resume books from the previously visited page.
- **🔄 Persistent Data Management:** Store and retrieve user-specific books, notes, highlights, and reading information.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Sign Up / Login:** Users access their personalized reading workspace.
2. **Upload Books:** PDF documents are uploaded and stored using Supabase Storage.
3. **Read & Explore:** Users read their books through the interactive PDF reader.
4. **Interact with Content:** Highlight text, create notes, explore word meanings, and access pronunciation.
5. **Save Progress:** Reading activities and progress are stored for future access.
6. **Continue Learning:** Users can revisit their saved books, notes, and highlights whenever they return.
